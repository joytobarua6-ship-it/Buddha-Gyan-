Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Buddha Gyan handles information when you interact with our Facebook Page and related Buddhist educational content.

Effective: 5 October 2026 Location: Bangladesh
Important: Buddha Gyan is a Facebook-based Buddhist educational and informational page. Facebook/Meta may independently collect and process information according to its own policies and settings.
1. Information We Collect 2. How We Use Information 3. Facebook & Third Parties 4. Comments & Messages 5. Children's Privacy 6. Changes to This Policy 7. Contact

1. Information We Collect

Buddha Gyan does not intentionally collect or store sensitive personal information from visitors.

When you interact with our Facebook Page, Facebook/Meta may collect information according to its own Privacy Policy and applicable settings. This may include public profile information, comments, reactions, messages, and other information you choose to provide when interacting with the Page.

2. How We Use Information

Information voluntarily provided through comments or messages may be used only for purposes such as:

  • Responding to questions or messages;
  • Communicating with followers;
  • Managing and moderating the Page;
  • Improving our educational and informational content; and
  • Maintaining a respectful and safe online community.

Buddha Gyan does not intentionally sell, rent, or trade users' personal information to third parties.

3. Facebook and Third-Party Services

Buddha Gyan operates primarily through Facebook and may use other third-party social media or online services.

Those services may collect and process information independently according to their own privacy policies and terms. Users are encouraged to review the privacy policies and terms of the services they use.

4. Comments and Messages

Please do not post or send sensitive personal information, passwords, financial information, identification documents, or other confidential information through the Buddha Gyan Facebook Page.

Information voluntarily shared publicly in comments may be visible to other Facebook users.

5. Children's Privacy

Buddha Gyan does not knowingly request or collect personal information from children for any specific purpose. Parents or guardians are encouraged to supervise children's online activities.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in our Page, services, or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be published through the appropriate online channel.

7. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the Buddha Gyan Facebook Page, please contact us through the official Buddha Gyan Facebook Page.
