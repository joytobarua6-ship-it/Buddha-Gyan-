About Buddha Gyan

Buddha Gyan is a Facebook-based Buddhist educational and informational page. The page shares Buddhist teachings, educational materials, inspirational content, quotations, and information related to Buddhism for educational and public-interest purposes.

Purpose of the Page

Buddha Gyan aims to make Buddhist teachings and related educational information accessible to a wider audience through social media.

  • Sharing Buddhist teachings and educational content.
  • Providing general information about Buddhist traditions, texts, and history.
  • Promoting reflection, compassion, wisdom, ethical conduct, and peaceful living.
  • Sharing publicly available Buddhist information for educational purposes.

Page Responsibility

Buddha Gyan is an independent social-media page and is not presented as a government organization or as an official representative of any particular Buddhist monastery, institution, or religious organization unless explicitly stated in a specific publication.

Important: Content shared by Buddha Gyan is intended for general educational and informational purposes. Where appropriate, users should consult qualified Buddhist teachers, recognized institutions, or original source materials for authoritative interpretation.

Contact

For questions, feedback, copyright concerns, or other inquiries concerning the Buddha Gyan Facebook Page, please contact us through the official Buddha Gyan Facebook Page.
